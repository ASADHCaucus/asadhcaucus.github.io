Bylaws of the American Studies Association Digital Humanities Caucus

The articles and bylaws of the ASA Digital Humanities Caucus are the organizations governing documents. They establish the structure, roles, and procedures for the caucus and guide its operations and decision-making processes and can be amended as needed to reflect the evolving needs of the caucus by its members.

ARTICLE I

The name of this organization shall be the Digital Humanities Caucus of the American Studies Association.

ARTICLE II

The purpose of the Caucus is to provide for discussion of professional issues and to promote research and activity of faculty, graduate students, librarians, and academic staff engaged in digital humanities work within the American Studies Association. The Advisory Council will advise the Caucus on matters that affect the academic or professional interests of the digital humanities scholars within the American Studies Association.

ARTICLE III. ELIGIBILITY FOR MEMBERSHIP

The voting membership of the DH Caucus shall consist of all persons who have signed up as caucus members, presented at the ASA, or attended the DH Caucus meeting at least once in the past 3 years. We recognize that precarious labor is a major issue for American Studies and particularly DH. As a result, those who help with projects (either as a part of the DH Caucus or were presented at the ASA), but could not necessarily attend the ASA in person, will also be considered voting members.

ARTICLE IV. EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE AND OFFICERS

Section 1.

The Caucus shall have an elected body of officers, which will be called the Advisory Council. This group shall consist of a Chair, Vice-Chair, four Members-at-Large, and one Past-Chair. The Members-at-Large, one position shall be a designated graduate student representative, and one shall be a non-tenure track member (academic staff, NTT faculty, librarian).

Section 2.

The Advisory Committee will call meetings, set the agenda for the meetings, form committees and task forces, and maintain a record of all Caucus proceedings. The Caucus will receive suggestions from Advisory Committee members and, when appropriate, refer these suggestions to the Caucus, or to the appropriate body. The Advisory Committee will inform the concerned member or group of its action, and will guard the anonymity of the member or group, if so requested.

Section 3. Terms of Office

The Vice Chair will be elected for a term of two years, the first year as Vice Chair and the subsequent year as Chair, and shall not be eligible to serve a consecutive term. The Secretary will be elected for one two-year term. The Secretary shall be Vice-Secretary the first year and Secretary the subsequent year. Members-at-Large will be elected for one two-year staggered term and may not serve on the Advisory Committee for more than two consecutive terms.

Section 4. Vacancies of Office

In the event that the Chair cannot complete the term of office, the Vice-Chair will assume the duties of the Chair. In the event that the Vice-Chair cannot complete a term of office, the Advisory Committee will select a Member-at-Large to fulfill the duties of that office. In the event that the Secretary cannot complete a term of office, the Vice- Secretary will assume the duties of that office. If a vacancy in the offices occurs more than six months before a general election, the Advisory Committee will call for the Elections Committee to administer a special election to fill that vacancy. If a vacancy occurs in the office of Member-at- Large less than six months before a general election, the Advisory Committee will appoint a member of a Caucus committee to fulfill the duties of that office.

Section 5. Duties of the Officers

  • The Chair The Chair will preside over meetings of the Advisory Committee, and meetings of the Caucus. The Chair is empowered to form committees to address issues raised in the Caucus, as suggested and approved by a two-thirds vote of a quorum of the members. The Chair is empowered to form task forces and working groups as suggested and approved by the Advisory Committee. The Chair shall appoint members to those committees, task forces, and working groups as gathered from a call for volunteers or suggested and approved by the members of theAdvisory Committee. The Chair is responsible for the budget of the Advisory Committee, setting the agenda for Advisory Committee and Caucus meetings, reporting committee and task force recommendations to the University Librarian, and representing the Caucus membership to other organizations.
  • The Vice Chair The Vice Chair shall serve in the absence of the Chair and shall succeed the Chair at the termination of the Chair's year in office. The Vice Chair shall serve as assistant to the Chair, carry out such duties as may be delegated by the Chair, and contribute to discussion and decisions of the Advisory Committee.
  • The Secretary The Secretary will maintain paper and electronic records of all transactions of the Caucus, and maintain the archives and online presence of the Caucus. This record shall be read to the Caucus and to the Advisory Committee as circumstances require. The Secretary shall announce the dates and times of the Advisory Committee meetings, and the Caucus meetings, through appropriate electronic channels. The Secretary is responsible for determining the number that is one-fourth of the Caucus membership. This is calculated at the beginning of every academic year.
  • The Vice-SecretaryThe Vice-Secretary shall serve in the absence of the Secretary and shall succeed the Secretary at the termination of the Secretary's year in office. The Vice-Secretary shall serve as assistant to the Secretary, carry out such duties as may be delegated by the Secretary, and contribute to discussion and decisions of the Advisory Committee.
  • The Members-at-Large The Members-at-Large will assist the Chair in carrying out the purposes of the Caucus, as the Chair may request. The Members-at-Large may serve on appointed committees, and serve as Advisory Committee liaisons to those committees. The Members-at-Large primary responsibility is to contribute to discussion and decisions of the Advisory Committee.

ARTICLE V. MEETINGS

Section 1. Disclosure of proceedings

Meetings of the Caucus are open to the public, except when the Advisory Committee deems it advisable to be closed. A summary of minutes shall be made available to the members electronically.

Section 2. Frequency of Meetings

Regular meetings of the Caucus shall be held once each month September through June. Regular meetings of the Advisory Committee shall be held at least once each month, and prior to the next Caucus meeting, in order to set the agenda.

Special meetings may be called at any time of the year by the Chair, upon approval of a majority of the Advisory Committee. The Advisory Committee can cancel meetings as it deems advisable.

Section 3. Quorum

One fourth of the membership shall constitute a quorum.

Section 2. Ad Hoc Committees and Task Forces and Working Groups

Any member of the Caucus may suggest the formation of a committee to consider matters affecting the members at large. Committees must be approved by a two-thirds vote of a Quorum. Committees may be dissolved upon recommendation of the Advisory Committee, and the dissolution must be approved by a majority vote of a Quorum. Members of committees shall be appointed by the Chair, as suggested and approved by members of the Advisory Committee.

Task forces are formed to research an issue. Working groups are formed to implement recommendations of committees and task forces. Task forces and working groups are formed by the Advisory Committee. Task forces and working groups are dissolved upon the completion of their charge. Members of task forces and working groups shall be appointed by the Chair, gathered from a call for volunteers or suggested and approved by members of theAdvisory Committee.

Each committee and task force and working group shall report to the Advisory Committee and continue in existence until it is discharged. Each committee and task force and working group may have a liaison and a chair.

The final report of the committee or task force or working group shall be reported to the membership. Findings and recommendations of committees and task forces and working groups are relayed to the Advisory Committee. The Advisory Committee shares relevant information with Executive Council.

ARTICLE VII. ELECTIONS

All members of the Caucus (see Article III) are eligible to be candidates for office and to vote in the annual elections.

Elections will be held during May of each year, except in extenuating circumstances. Nominations will be solicited from the membership through the Caucus listserv. The Elections Committee will administer the election, and report the results to the Caucus.

The Elections Committee, following procedures in Robert's Rules of Order, will call for nominations to the elected offices of the Caucus, ascertain the nominees' willingness to serve, prepare a list of nominees, and distribute and count ballots.

The nominees shall be elected by a plurality of members casting ballots. The ballots will list the offices as Vice- Chair/Chair-Elect, Vice-Secretary/Secretary-Elect, and Members-at-Large. Members may vote for two Members-at-Large each year to replace the two whose terms are ending. The Members-at-Large will be the candidates with the two highest tallies for the office. The newly-elected Advisory Committee will take office at the conclusion of the June Caucus meeting of the election year.

ARTICLE VIII. PARLIAMENTARY AUTHORITY

The Caucus is governed by the provisions of the Bylaws, any rules the Caucus may establish, and any rules, regulations, or statutes applicable to the Caucus. On matters not otherwise defined, the current issue of Robert’s Rules of Order shall govern.

ARTICLE IX. AMENDMENT AND RATIFICATION OF THE BYLAWS

Any member of the Caucus may suggest amendments. Amendments shall require approval by at least two-thirds of the Caucus members who respond to a ballot sent to the entire Caucus membership. Suggested amendments must be introduced to the members at least one meeting prior to the meeting where a vote of approval will be called. The vote of approval will be conducted by secret ballot. Written copies of the suggested amendments will be provided to any members who request them.
